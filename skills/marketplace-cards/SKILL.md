# Marketplace Cards Generator

## Overview
Use this skill to design the sequence of image cards for an e-commerce product listing. Effective marketplace cards tell a visual story, answer FAQs, and drive conversions directly from the image carousel.

## Workflow

### 1. Structure the Carousel
A standard highly-converting carousel consists of 6-7 images:
1.  **Main Image:** Pure white background, high resolution, product takes up 85% of the frame.
2.  **The Hook / Big Benefit:** Product in use with a massive, readable headline stating the #1 benefit.
3.  **Features Callout:** Infographic style pointing to 3-4 specific features of the product.
4.  **Size / Scale / What's Included:** Visual representation of dimensions or unboxing layout.
5.  **Social Proof / Before & After:** Visual evidence of results, or a prominent customer quote.
6.  **Comparison Chart (Us vs. Them):** Checkmarks for our product, X's for competitors.
7.  **Lifestyle / Emotional Payoff:** Aspirational image of the target avatar enjoying the product.

### 2. Generate the Assets
For each of the 7 cards above, provide:
- **Visual Description:** What the image should look like (for the designer or AI image generator).
- **Copy / Text Overlay:** The exact text that should be overlaid on the image. Keep it under 15 words per card.
