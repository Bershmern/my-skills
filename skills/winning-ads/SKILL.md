# Winning Ads Framework

## Overview
A "Winning Ad" is a creative that scales successfully in paid media. This skill breaks down the anatomy of winning ads and helps the user generate new variations based on proven structures.

## Workflow

### 1. The Anatomy of a Winning Ad
Remind the user of the 4 key components:
1.  **The Hook (0-3s):** Grabs attention visually and auditorily.
2.  **The Problem/Agitation (3-10s):** Validates the viewer's pain point.
3.  **The Solution/Mechanism (10-25s):** Introduces the product and explains *how* it works (the unique mechanism).
4.  **The Call to Action (25-30s):** Tells them exactly what to do next with an irresistible offer.

### 2. Generate Ad Angles
Based on `products-and-positioning.md`, generate 3 distinct Winning Ad angles:
- **Angle 1: The Founder Story.** (Authentic, low-production, speaking directly to camera about why the product was created).
- **Angle 2: The TikTok Trend/Meme.** (Leveraging a current audio or visual trend to natively integrate the product).
- **Angle 3: The "Things I Wish I Knew" Listicles.** (Educational format that subtly positions the product as the ultimate solution).

### 3. Scripting
- Ask the user which angle they prefer.
- Write a full, timestamped script for a 30-45 second video, detailing both visual actions and spoken audio.
