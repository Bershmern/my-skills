# Generate 50

## Overview
Quality comes from quantity. When the user needs fresh angles or ideas, use this skill to aggressively generate exactly 50 distinct outputs.

## Workflow

### 1. Establish the Goal
- Confirm exactly what we are generating 50 of (e.g., ad headlines, email subject lines, content ideas, product features).

### 2. Apply the 5x10 Matrix
To ensure variety, divide the 50 ideas into 5 distinct categories or angles (10 ideas per category).
Examples of categories for ad copy:
- Category 1: Fear / Pain avoidance
- Category 2: Logic / Cost savings
- Category 3: Aspirational / Status
- Category 4: Direct / Highly descriptive
- Category 5: Curiosity / Bizarre

### 3. Generate and Output
- Produce a numbered list from 1 to 50.
- Do not add fluff or lengthy explanations. Keep the list punchy and scannable.

### 4. Refinement
- Ask the user to pick their top 3-5 favorites for further refinement or expansion.
