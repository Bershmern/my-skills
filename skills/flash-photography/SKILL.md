# Flash Photography Aesthetic

## Overview
The "direct flash" or "paparazzi style" photography aesthetic is highly effective for Gen-Z and modern DTC brands. It feels raw, authentic, and native to social media platforms. Use this skill to generate assets in this specific style.

## Workflow

### 1. Understand the Aesthetic
The core elements of this style include:
- Harsh, direct on-camera flash.
- Deep, dark, or underexposed backgrounds.
- High contrast and high saturation.
- Slight film grain, disposable camera look (e.g., 35mm film, point-and-shoot).
- Often features motion blur or slightly candid, unposed framing.

### 2. Craft the Prompts
- Ask the user what product or subject they are advertising.
- Generate 3-5 prompts using the following keywords: `direct on-camera flash`, `harsh lighting`, `point and shoot camera`, `disposable camera aesthetic`, `35mm film photograph`, `high contrast`, `candid`.

### 3. Example Prompt Output
- *Example:* "A candid 35mm film photograph of a young woman laughing while holding a bright pink energy drink can. Direct on-camera flash, harsh lighting, deep shadows behind her, dark background, point-and-shoot camera aesthetic, raw and authentic, party atmosphere."
