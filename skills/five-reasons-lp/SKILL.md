# Five Reasons Landing Page

## Overview
The "Five Reasons Why [Target Audience] Loves [Product]" is a classic, high-converting advertorial and landing page format. Use this skill to draft the structure and copy for such a page.

## Workflow

### 1. Identify the Five Core Pillars
- Review `products-and-positioning.md`.
- Extract the 5 strongest selling points, benefits, or features that solve the avatar's biggest problems.

### 2. Draft the Structure
Follow this exact page structure:
1.  **Headline:** "5 Reasons Why [Avatar] Are Obsessed With [Product]"
2.  **Introduction:** A short empathetic hook relating to the core problem.
3.  **Reason 1 (The Big Hook):** The most impactful benefit.
4.  **Reason 2 (The Differentiator):** Why this is better than the competition.
5.  **Reason 3 (The Logic/Science):** How it actually works.
6.  **Reason 4 (The Social Proof):** Highlighting community or expert validation.
7.  **Reason 5 (The Risk Reversal):** Price, guarantee, and ease of use.
8.  **Conclusion & CTA:** A strong push to buy now with a compelling offer.

### 3. Write the Copy
- Draft the copy for each section using the brand's tone of voice.
- Keep paragraphs short (1-3 sentences) for readability.
