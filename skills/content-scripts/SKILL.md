# Content Scripts (@bershmern)

Use this skill when tasked with drafting content scripts or ideas for the personal channel (@bershmern) aimed at building audience and driving traffic to Noble Casca.

## Guidelines
1. **Rooted in Reality**: Ideas/scripts must be rooted in real pain points per tier (pull from `project-docs/brand-source-of-truth.md`), not generic "founder content."
2. **Tier Mapping**: Every piece of content should map to one tier's "world." Do not write generic content that doesn't point toward a specific service (Brand, Product & Web, or Studio Partner).
3. **Memory Discipline**: Track which scripts got used/posted in `project-docs/session-log.md` so you do not repeat ideas.
